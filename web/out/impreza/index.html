2024 Impreza oil

How oil moves through the 2.0 L FB20 BOXER, what to pour, how much, and when it is consumed between changes. Specs from the 2024 Subaru Impreza Owner’s Manual — not Last Reading.

Selected3. Oil filter. Replace the filter at every oil change. Manual fill with filter is 4.7 US qt (4.4 L) — a guideline; always finish on the dipstick.

How to check

  1. Park on a level surface and stop the engine.
  2. Wait at least 5 minutes so oil drains back to the pan.
  3. Pull the level gauge, wipe it, and seat it fully until it stops.
  4. Read both sides — the true level is the lower mark.
  5. If below L, add 0W-16 slowly through the filler. L to F is about 1.1 US qt (1 L). Do not go above F when cold.

Between changes — oil is used

Some engine oil is consumed while driving. Under the conditions below, Subaru says to check at least every second fuel fill and change more often. Different drivers in the same car can see different results.

  • New engine / break-in
  • Wrong viscosity or lower-quality oil
  • Repeated engine braking
  • High RPM for long stretches
  • Heavy load for long stretches
  • Long idle
  • Stop-and-go / heavy traffic
  • Severe heat or cold
  • Frequent accel / decel

What to pour

Preferred: Subaru-approved 0W-16 synthetic, ILSAC GF-6B (shield mark). If that can is not on the shelf, ILSAC GF-6A (starburst) or API SP Resource Conserving is the listed alternative. If 0W-16 is unavailable, 0W-20 may be used to top off, then return to 0W-16 at the next change.

US 2024 Impreza is the 2.0 L FB20. The same oil spec is printed for the 2.5 L FB25 if that engine appears on the vehicle. CVT fluid (FB20 models 10.8 US qt) and front/rear differential gear oil are not engine oil.
